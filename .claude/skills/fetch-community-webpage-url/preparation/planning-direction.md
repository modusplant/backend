# Planning Direction
## Target Users
- Primary: people who like flowers and plants and enjoy recording their own plant life or browsing others' records — plant keepers, light keepers with 1–3 plants, newcomers, and people who photograph flowers and plants in daily life.
- Secondary: first-time growers and people who need plant information — searchers of specific care methods, gift recipients, and those who want real growers' experience.
- Plant ownership is not a precondition for participation.

## Core Value
- Record: photo/video records of everyday flowers and plants, accumulated into a personal green archive (private by default, public by choice).
- Discover: browse other users' public records and highlights, react with "applause" rather than likes.
- Information: plant encyclopedia that explains a plant's native environment and the reason behind each care step, summarized as shareable info cards.
- Experience sharing: community with Tip / Q&A / Daily categories, posts linked to growing-environment info (e.g. light direction, pot type, years grown).
